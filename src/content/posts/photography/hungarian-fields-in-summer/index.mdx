During the summer holidays, we decided to visit Hungary and travel a bit here
and there. We rented a car so I could practice a little bit as well. I got my
driving license only recently, and it was a good time to put some real time into
driving skills. I also had my camera with me and took a few photos. I used Kodak
100-8 (Kodacolor 100) with my Olympus OM-2N and a default Zuiko MC 50mm f/1.8
lens.

It feels good to finally gain independence with transportation. I like to think
that I can stop the car at any time, get out and take a photo, or just rest.
This is what we did on that trip. We drove around, stopping from time to time
and making photos, mostly fields and sunflowers, but once we also drove into a
small village and explored traditional houses and a very pleasant forest trail.

I was mostly working from home during that short trip, but on weekends and in
the evenings I took small adventures around. It was hot and quiet on the roads,
and the wind was whispering in the tall grass. I think I've never seen that many
sunflowers concentrated in the same place at one time!
