## Gardens & Ruins

This website is my small digital garden: a place for essays, photographs, and
illustrations that I want to keep in a quiet, readable form. I wanted it to feel
like a notebook made for the web, simple, intentional, and a little atmospheric.

It is built with Astro 7 and TypeScript, with content written in MDX and
organized through Astro content collections. The styling is handled with Sass,
while EB Garamond Variable lends the site its more literary, editorial feel and
Inter Variable provides the cleaner supporting text. I also keep the project
grounded with the usual tools for linting, formatting, and production builds, so
the experience remains both polished and easy to maintain.

The site is designed to support a modest publishing workflow: posts and pages
live in structured content collections and the website is hosted on Vercel,
which makes the whole thing feel lightweight and straightforward in practice.
